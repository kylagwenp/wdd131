Keep it simple

Your first painting kit

  • A small canvas or paper suited to your paint
  • Blue, red, yellow, and white paint
  • One flat brush and one round brush
  • A palette or reusable mixing surface
  • Water, an old cloth, and a protected table

Starting with watercolor? Choose watercolor paper and a pointed round brush. You can mix many greens from blue and yellow.
